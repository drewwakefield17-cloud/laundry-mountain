Editing plan, sound and source notes

Remotion’s job: keep app footage full-frame, make controlled camera moves into the relevant controls, carry the confirmed count into the progress payoff, time cuts to voice/music, and preserve the exact app type, colours and artwork. It is the editing tool; it does not supply the story.

Sound: dry delivery at the opening; a light percussive climb begins with Basky; a quiet tap at confirmation; an upward accent with the movement; one badge chime; a resolved final beat. No music is selected or generated for this storyboard.

Source: supplied owner-session-02.mp4, exact sample positions 0, 17.5, 22, 25.2 and 42 seconds, browser chrome cropped. These are manual counts. 200 m does not reach the first 250 m checkpoint, so this board does not pretend it does.

Motion feasibility: core product motion already exists in the recording. Opening/closing use compositing and camera moves from approved artwork. A new photoreal cinematic walking shot would be a separate asset task; no new generation or credit spend is started.
