## Shared treatment

- Primary buttons: emerald gradient, restrained top highlight, shallow dark lower
  edge and a small pressed movement; consistent across the app. Secondary controls
  remain quieter. Maintain contrast, touch targets and reduced-motion handling.
- Cards: deliberate grouping and subtle depth; avoid a border around every piece
  of text. Reward cards may have warmer accents without claiming unearned rewards.
- Type/space: confident compact headings and readable supporting text; reduce
  large purposeless gaps. Retain generous space around the primary action.
- Art: preserve approved basket, badges and active climb. Use scenic art where
  it supports the screen, not as a universal decoration. No fresh mascot redesign.
